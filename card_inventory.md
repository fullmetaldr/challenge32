# MTG Card Inventory Estimate

_Last updated: 25 September 2026_

This document is a rough census of the physical Magic: The Gathering collection. It is **not** an exact inventory. The purpose is to preserve a defensible estimate of collection size and the assumptions behind it so the figures can be updated later.

## Headline estimate

| Measure | Low estimate | Best estimate | High estimate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total physical cards currently accounted for | ~12,000 | **~14,600** | ~17,500 |
| Playable cards, excluding known token/art-card boxes | ~11,000 | **~13,600** | ~16,400 |

**Working estimate:** approximately **14,500–15,000 physical cards**, of which roughly **13,500–14,000 are playable game cards**.

This is probably still conservative because several known sources of cards are not included in the numerical total.

---

## 1. Bundle boxes / set bulk

The April 2026 shelf photo contains **25 Bundle-sized boxes**. Approximately **4–5 additional Bundle boxes** have been added since then.

Current estimated box count:

- Low: 29 boxes
- Midpoint: 29.5 boxes
- High: 30 boxes

A standard Bundle box is assumed to hold approximately **500–550 unsleeved cards** when full.

Estimated occupancy:

- **50–60%** of boxes are packed full.
- Another **15%** are approximately **25–60% full**.
- The remainder are essentially empty.
- At least **two full boxes** contain tokens/art cards rather than normal playable cards.

### Bundle-box calculation

#### Low estimate

- 29 boxes
- 50% full = 14.5 equivalent full boxes
- 15% at 25% occupancy = 1.0875 equivalent full boxes
- Total equivalent full boxes = 15.5875
- Capacity assumption = 500 cards

`15.5875 × 500 ≈ 7,794 cards`

Rounded: **~7,800 cards**

#### Midpoint estimate

- 29.5 boxes
- 55% full = 16.225 equivalent full boxes
- 15% at midpoint occupancy of 42.5% = 1.8806 equivalent full boxes
- Total equivalent full boxes = 18.1056
- Capacity assumption = 525 cards

`18.1056 × 525 ≈ 9,505 cards`

Rounded: **~9,500 cards**

#### High estimate

- 30 boxes
- 60% full = 18 equivalent full boxes
- 15% at 60% occupancy = 2.7 equivalent full boxes
- Total equivalent full boxes = 20.7
- Capacity assumption = 550 cards

`20.7 × 550 = 11,385 cards`

Rounded: **~11,400 cards**

### Tokens and art cards

At least two full Bundle boxes are known to contain tokens/art cards.

| Estimate | Token/art cards |
|---|---:|
| Low | ~1,000 |
| Midpoint | ~1,050 |
| High | ~1,100 |

These cards are already included in the Bundle-box totals above. They are subtracted only when estimating the number of playable cards.

At the midpoint, the Bundle boxes therefore contain approximately:

- **~9,500 total physical cards**
- **~8,450 playable cards**
- **~1,050 tokens/art cards**

## 6. The Lost Children™

Loose or semi-organised cards exist in boosters, old cardboard deck boxes, overflow piles, and other temporary locations.

Examples include:

- cards from sets whose Bundle box is already full;
- valuable commons/uncommons that were never filed;
- mixed booster bulk;
- cards opened when there was no energy/time to organise them;
- cards removed from partially dismantled precons;
- mechanically specific cards deliberately kept separate because there is no obvious permanent home.

A concrete example is the remaining card pool from the **Squirreled Away** precon after it evolved into the APD deck. Many squirrel-specific cards were retained because they may become relevant again, but there is no dedicated Bloomburrow storage box.

Working estimate:

- Low: ~200
- Midpoint: **~450**
- High: ~800

This is one of the least reliable categories and could be materially larger.

## 9. Known card sources not included in the total

The estimate above does **not** currently include several known pools of cards:

- Several preconstructed Commander decks in **limbo**:
  - partially dismantled;
  - awaiting possible promotion into the active roster;
  - neither fully counted as Commander decks nor reliably included in bulk.
- Several additional small boxes of bulk intended to be sold, donated, or otherwise removed from the collection.
- Any other forgotten deck boxes, booster packs containing loose cards, overflow piles, or temporary storage not covered above.
- Future Reality Fracture purchases and other cards acquired after this census.

Because these sources are real but unquantified, the **true physical collection is likely larger than the ~14,600 midpoint**.

A total exceeding **15,000 cards** is therefore entirely plausible.

---

## 10. Inventory implications

At a midpoint estimate of roughly **13,600 playable cards**, individually scanning the entire collection would be impractical.

For perspective:

- At 5 seconds per card: ~18.9 hours of uninterrupted scanning.
- At 10 seconds per card: ~37.8 hours.
- These figures exclude recognition errors, duplicate handling, organisation, database correction, and returning cards to storage.

A future full inventory would therefore benefit from:

1. Batch image recognition rather than one-card-at-a-time scanning.
2. Processing the collection by storage unit/set.
3. Recording duplicates automatically.
4. Treating already-built Commander decks as separate known lists where possible.
5. Incrementally processing new purchases and booster pulls so the inventory becomes more complete over time rather than requiring a single large census.
