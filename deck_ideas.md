# Deck ideas

Potential Challenge32 decks. These are ideas to explore, not tracked decklists or
commitments to build.

## Mono-blue — Urza, Lord High Artificer

- **Motivation:** I own a particularly cool foil Urza printing and do not yet
  have a mono-blue deck.
- **Direction:** Artefact-heavy. The specific strategy is still open.
- **Starting point:** Consider repurposing cards from the existing
  [Karn, Legacy Reforged deck](https://moxfield.com/decks/6FUZSlRIN0-9rSt8ENrGOA).
- **To decide:** What should the deck actually do beyond playing artefacts?

## Witch-Maw — Atraxa, Praetors' Voice

- **Direction:** Make use of proliferate, probably through planeswalkers,
  Sagas, poison counters, and charge counters.
- **Preference:** Avoid a +1/+1-counter focus; tracking those counters is
  annoying.
- **To decide:** Which of those proliferate themes should be central?

## Mono-green — Fynn, the Fangbearer

- **Motivation:** I own the *FINAL FANTASY: Through the Ages* printing (Vayne
  Carudas Solidor) and several green creatures that could support it.
- **Direction:** Deathtouch creatures dealing combat damage to players, with
  Fynn adding two poison counters each time. This is not the toxic 2 ability.
- **To decide:** Which support creatures and other tools make this a satisfying
  mono-green deck?

## Rakdos — commander undecided

- **Direction:** A chaotic deck, possibly focused on goad or on punishing
  opponents for their actions.
- **To decide:** Choose between those approaches, then choose a commander.
  Several candidates are under consideration, but none has been selected.
